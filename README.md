## 🎯 Shooter Stats Tracker

A clean and lightweight web app to track your performance across all your favorite shooter games.
Create separate game sections, record your stats, and watch your progress level up.
Perfect for players who want a simple, organized way to monitor improvement.


---

## ✨ Features

✔️ Create custom sections for different shooter games
✔️ Track essential stats with clean UI
✔️ Works smoothly on mobile + desktop


---

## 🎮 Why This Exists

Shooter players hop between multiple games — this tracker gives you one place to store everything.
No clutter. No over-complication. Just your stats, your progress, your improvement.
